# MyMemories

### A visual home for the places and moments you bring back with you.

<!-- Add a hero screenshot at docs/screenshots/album-overview.png. -->
![MyMemories album overview](docs/screenshots/album-overview.png)

MyMemories turns a collection of postcards into a living travel archive. Save the image, the story, the place, and the date behind each postcard, then revisit the collection as an album or across an interactive map.

The experience is currently centered on postcards, with room to grow into other kinds of souvenirs and keepsakes.

## Keep every detail of the memory

| | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Build your collection** | Add postcards from your camera or gallery and give each one a title, country, city, region, description, and location. |
| **Make every image feel right** | Crop and rotate the original image, then prepare a dedicated 3:2 cover for the album. |
| **Browse like an album** | View your postcards in a visual grid, search by title, and filter by place or date. |
| **See the journey** | Explore postcard markers on a world map, with clusters for places where memories meet. |
| **Open the full story** | Flip into a postcard detail view, review its metadata, update it, replace its images, or remove it. |
| **Make it yours** | Create a personal account and add an optional profile picture. |

## From postcard to memory

1. **Bring it in.** Choose a photo from your gallery or take one with your camera.
2. **Frame it.** Adjust the original image and select the cover that represents it in your album.
3. **Place it in time and space.** Add as much date precision as you know and choose the location on the map.
4. **Add the story.** Give the postcard a title and keep a note about the moment behind it.
5. **Return to it.** Find it in the album, or follow the map back to the place where it began.

## The idea

Travel memories are often stored in boxes, drawers, and camera rolls. MyMemories gives them a place that is easier to revisit: part album, part map, and part personal journal.

## Project status

MyMemories is a personal project focused on making physical souvenirs easier to preserve, browse, and remember.
